Employment Terms & Conditions

{{ employee.full_name | upper }}

1. SALARY / ALLOWANCE
  • Your salary / allowance as mutually agreed will be as stated in the cover letter.
  • The salary / allowance will be paid on the last day of each month.
  • The management does not allow advance payment of salary or as loan.
  • The salary will be transferred to your personal account that has been given to us by you.
2. PROBATION PERIOD

Your probation period is based on stated on the offer letter.

3. WORKING HOURS

Your working hours will be as per the timetable determined by the office. The management has the discretion to change the work time and location maintaining the same hours of work. Any excess of hours will be paid accordingly as mutually agreed.

4. ATTIRE AND GROOMING
  • Maintained good hair care and well groomed not being over adorned.
  • Maintain clean personal hygiene.
  • Dress decent and smart with collared shirt, decent T-Shirt, decent pants and fully attired with shoes.
  • Round-neck T-shirts, slippers, sandals and sport suits are not allowed.
5. ANNUAL LEAVE
  • Leave application must be submitted before 10 working days.
  • Leave approval is on the discretion of the management.
  • Leave entitlement will be calculated on a pro-rated basis.
  • Unused leave can be carried forward to the subsequent year for a period of 2 years.
  • Evidence of pregnancy is to be informed with confirmation of pregnancy.
6. MEDICAL LEAVE
  • Use your wise discretion during illness and when on medical leave.
  • Please provide valid medical chit if on medical leave.
  • You are advised not to abuse medical leave to disturb our service.
7. PUBLIC HOLIDAY
  • Entitlement of public holidays as declared by State of Selangor and Government of Malaysia.
8. LAWFUL DEDUCTION

The following lawful deduction will be made from your salary wherever applicable:

  • Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) — employee's contribution as per the EPF Act 1991.
  • Social Security Organisation (SOCSO) — employee's contribution as per the Employees' Social Security Act 1969.
  • Employment Insurance System (EIS) — employee's contribution as per the Employment Insurance System Act 2017.
  • Income tax (PCB/MTD) if applicable, as per the Income Tax Act 1967.
  • Overpayment of salaries and other benefits.
  • Other deductions as freely consented in writing or as stated in this contract.
9. CONDUCT

The employee at all times is subjected to rules and regulations and other administrative instructions as may from time to time be issued related to work conduct and conditions of the service in the Company.

10. NOTICE OF TERMINATION
  • Period of termination notice after the probation period applicable to both parties will be {{ notice_period_days }} ({{ notice_period_days | int | string | upper }} ) days during the period of employment, failing which the defaulter party has to pay in lieu of the days not fulfilled. This does not apply on dismissal due to indiscipline, misconduct or breach of contract.
  • Termination or resignation other than written form will not be accepted.
  • Any dues payable to the company will be deducted from the last salary.
11. TERMINATION WITHOUT NOTICE BY EMPLOYER

The Employment agreement maybe terminated by the Company without due notice if the employee:

  • Willfully disobeys the lawful and reasonable order on demand or request by the Management.
  • Is guilty of fraud or dishonesty.
  • Contravenes any article of this contract or willfully breaches any conditions of the contract.
  • Convicted to any serious crime by the court of Malaysia or any other countries.
  • The Management is dissatisfied with the performance of the employee and as such has been notified by verbal or written warning.
  • Involves in any action or words directly or indirectly which is detrimental to the income, reputation and status of the Company or its Directors and staff.
12. WAIVER OF BREACH

The waiver by the Company of a Breach of any provision of this Agreement by the employee shall not operate or be construed as a waiver of any subsequent breach by the employee. No waiver shall be valid unless it is in writing and signed by the Directors. The Management shall after due inquiring, take disciplinary action against the said employee for breach of regulations, negligence of duty, unsatisfactory performance, rudeness or any action which may diversely affect the Company's image and reputation.

13. MEDICAL AND PHYSICAL STATUS

The offer of this Employment is subject to the declaration made by you through the application form or your physical and mental state of fitness. Should it be found after employment of non-disclosure, inter-alia, material facts regarding any illness, medical or physical disability, it shall be deemed to be sufficient cause for the Company to terminate your service and you shall not be liable to any claims for benefit there after.

14. RESTRICTIONS OF CERTAIN ACTIONS
  • Employee should be loyal to the Company and avoid discussing the Company's matter in public. Employee should ensure that all confidential papers are safely locked.
  • Not to communicate in any manner to the press and other persons, the trade secrets, financial position or other affairs concerning the company, its management or employees.
  • You are NOT to operate your own or employed by other company or through agents serve as a professional consultant or related services near our business premises within a radius of 8 km, failing which legal proceeding will be instituted against you.
15. PROFESSIONAL LIABILITY
  • All attempts should be made to adhere strictly to the medical code of ethics towards the public, colleagues, subordinates and business associate.
  • Personal indemnity insurance and medical protection must be made available at your own cost and its compulsory as part of our employment contract.
  • The company does not bear any liability to individual breach of ethics and any litigations arising out of your actions during or after working hours.

18. PARKING, TOLL, MILEAGE AND OTHER CLAIM
  • Parking allowance is fixed at RM100.00 monthly for all staff stationed at the Cyberjaya office.
  • Parking allowance will be paid along with staff's monthly salary.
  • Parking fee for other locations during working visits will be paid per receipt amount.
  • Parking fee that is more than RM30 per day is required management approval.
  • Toll is claim per receipt amount. Only toll fee that is incurred for working visits will be approved.
  • Mileage claim for working visit: First 100 KM at RM0.70 per KM; Next remaining KM at RM0.50 per KM.
  • Parking, toll and mileage cost for daily commute from staff's home and Cyberjaya office are not eligible for claim.
  • Any claim reimbursement will be processed in 14 days.

  • Any need for emergency leave will have to be consulted with the office or immediate manager.
